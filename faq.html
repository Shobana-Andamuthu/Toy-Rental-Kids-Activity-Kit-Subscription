Subscription Questions

JoyLoop is a monthly toy rental subscription. You select a plan (Starter, Play Plus, or Adventure), pick toys from our library, receive a sanitized delivery box, and swap them out whenever your child is ready for the next adventure.

Yes! You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time with 1 click in your account dashboard before your next renewal date.

Toy Rotation & Selection

Absolutely. You can customize your box from our catalog or opt for our expert developmental curation based on your child's age profile.

Returns & Shipping

Yes, return shipping is 100% free. Every delivery box contains a pre-paid, pre-printed shipping label.

Hygiene & Safety

Every toy passes through our 6-step medical sanitization protocol: enzymatic wash, high-temp steam, and 15 minutes of medical UV-C light sterilization.

Billing & Payments

Your subscription is billed monthly on the date you signed up. Annual billing plans are charged once per year at a 20% discount.

Box Swaps

You can purchase any item from your active box at a discounted member price directly from your parent account dashboard!
