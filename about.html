Our Purpose & Story

Reimagining How Children Play

We founded JoyLoop on a simple belief: Children don't need more plastic clutter—they need better ways to explore, create, and outgrow their toys sustainably.

Sustainable Circular Economy

By sharing premium wooden toys across families, we keep thousands of tons of plastic waste out of landfills.

Clutter-Free Living

Transform messy playrooms into organized, serene spaces by rotating toys when your child loses interest.

Pediatric Skill Curation

Every box is chosen alongside child educators to target specific cognitive, sensory, and motor milestones.

Affordable Access

Access thousands of dollars worth of award-winning heirloom toys for a small monthly subscription fee.

How It Started

Born from Two Parents Searching for a Better Way

In 2024, our founders watched their 3-year-old spend 20 minutes playing with a $150 luxury wooden play set before abandoning it forever.

Realizing that 80% of purchased toys end up forgotten under beds or in trash bins within 60 days, we set out to build a subscription platform that gives kids fresh discovery while respecting parent budgets and our planet.
