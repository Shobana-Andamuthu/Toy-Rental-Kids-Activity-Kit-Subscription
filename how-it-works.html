Simple 6-Step Journey

Endless Play. Zero Clutter.

JoyLoop replaces crowded living rooms with a rotating cycle of discovery. Here is how our hassle-free monthly subscription works for your family.

Choose Your Plan Browse Library

The JoyLoop Rotation Cycle

Designed for maximum fun for kids and maximum convenience for parents.

01

1. Select Your Subscription Plan

Choose from Starter, Play Plus, or Adventure based on how many toys your child wants to explore each month. Cancel or pause anytime with 1 click.

02

2. Pick Your Favorite Toy Kits

Browse our online catalog filtered by age (0–12 yrs) and developmental milestones. Select specific toys or let our pediatric experts curate a surprise box.

03

3. Receive Sanitized Eco-Box at Your Door

Your customized kit arrives at your doorstep inside our reusable shipping container. Every single toy is 100% hospital-grade UV-C sanitized and sealed in organic bags.

04

4. Play, Explore & Build Skills

Enjoy 30 full days of creative, unrestricted playtime. Use our parent guide cards for fun learning activities and milestone tracking.

05

5. Pack & Drop Off with Pre-Paid Label

When your month is up, simply place the toys back into the same reusable box. Apply the included pre-paid return shipping label and drop it off at any parcel point.

06

6. Discover Your Next Month's Adventures

Your next curated batch arrives just as the old one returns—keeping your child continuously engaged with fresh developmental challenges.

Parent Peace of Mind
