Step-by-Step Parent Flow

The Complete Parent Journey

From selecting your child's age to monthly toy swaps and billing management, here is your complete journey.

01

1. Choose Child's Age

Select your child's age group (0–2 Yrs, 3–5 Yrs, 6–8 Yrs, or 9–12 Yrs) to unlock developmental-stage specific toys.

02

2. Browse Toys & Activity Kits

Explore hundreds of premium wooden toys, STEM coding companions, sensory kits, and open-ended building sets.

03

3. Filter by Age & Category

Use clear filters (Educational, Pretend Play, Outdoor, Building Sets, STEM) to find exactly what fits your child's mood today.

04

4. Choose Subscription Plan

Select Starter, Play Plus, or Adventure tier based on your monthly box volume. Flexible billing with 1-click pause or cancel.

05

5. Receive Sanitized Toys

Receive your sanitized eco-box right at your doorstep. Every single item is hospital-grade UV-C sterilized and eco-sealed.

06

6. Manage Current Rentals

Log in to your parent dashboard to view active kits in your home, track remaining days, and check sanitization certificates.

07

7. Request a Swap

When your child is ready for new challenges, click "Request Swap" from your portal to queue next month's selection.

08

8. Next Monthly Rotation

Pack previous toys back into the reusable container using the included pre-paid return label as your new box arrives.

09

9. View Billing & Invoices

Easily view payment receipts, download PDF invoices, and update billing methods anytime from your account portal.
