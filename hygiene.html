100% Hospital-Grade Clean

Cleaner Than Brand New

Your child's health is our absolute priority. Every single toy that enters our library passes through a rigorous 6-step medical sanitization protocol before it ever reaches your home.

JoyLoop Clean Certificate UV-C Sterilized & Eco-Sealed

Our 6-Step Hygiene Sequence

The exact process every toy undergoes between child rotations.

01
1. Return Inspection

Upon return, toys are individually unboxed in our clean facility and checked by hand for structural integrity, missing parts, or material wear.

02
2. Organic Enzymatic Wash

Toys undergo a deep surface wash using plant-based, non-toxic enzymatic cleansers that break down organic matter without harsh chemicals.

03
3. Medical-Grade UV-C Chamber

Toys spend 15 minutes in our medical UV-C light sterilization chamber, neutralizing 99.9% of bacteria, viruses, and germ spores.

04
4. High-Temp Steam Disinfection

For wooden grooves, fabric parts, and crevices, high-temperature micro-steam penetrates hard-to-reach areas without chemical residue.

05
5. Safety & Quality Audit

A quality specialist verifies smooth edges, secure battery compartments, and tight seams before certifying with a Clean Stamp.

06
6. Sealed Eco Packaging

Sanitized toys are sealed inside reusable, breathable organic cotton bags inside our shipping container, ready for your home.
